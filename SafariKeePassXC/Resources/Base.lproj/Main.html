Getting started

  1. Install and start KeePassXC. In Settings → Browser Integration, turn on “Enable browser integration”.
  2. Unlock your database in KeePassXC.
  3. Turn on the extension in Safari → Settings → Extensions and allow website access.
  4. Click the extension icon in Safari and choose “Connect to KeePassXC”. Confirm and name the connection in KeePassXC.

Important: This app is the bridge between Safari and KeePassXC - it must stay open for the extension to work. The window may be closed.
